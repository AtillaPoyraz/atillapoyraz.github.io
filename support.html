Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Report an inappropriate ad

Email us with the ad's name or advertiser, when it appeared, and what was inappropriate. Include a screenshot if available. You can also use Settings → Report an ad in the game.

Why is revive unavailable?

A rewarded revive requires an available ad and an internet connection. Ad availability can vary. You can always restart without watching an ad.

Where is my progress saved?

Coins, unlocked balls, preferences and your best score are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove this progress. Game Center scores are separate from your local coin balance.

Rankings and offline play

You can play without signing in. Sign in through your iPhone's Game Center settings to use rankings. Your local best is checked for synchronization when you return to the game or open rankings.
