This policy describes Bounce Protocol for iOS and this support website, provided by Atilla Poyraz. Contact: atilla.poyraz1998@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game stores your best score, coins, unlocked and selected balls, daily quest progress, tutorial status and sound and vibration preferences on your device. It also stores successful leaderboard submissions by Game Center player identifier to avoid duplicate submissions. We do not operate a server to collect this local progress.

Optional Game Center

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, the game submits your best score to its leaderboard using your Game Center player identity. Apple processes Game Center information under Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center display name and score may be visible to other players according to Apple's settings.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent choices. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Processing depends on your consent choices, device settings and region.

Where required, a consent form is presented. The in-game Privacy entry is available when Google requires privacy options. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. Tracking or access to the advertising identifier requires the applicable iOS permission; declining does not prevent normal play.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps for its processing, retention and privacy controls.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request or investigate the reported issue. Please send only information needed to describe the problem. Support email is processed through Gmail.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. Hosting is intended for GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement. Following external links takes you to services with their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until removed with the app or device data; device backups may retain copies. Game Center and Google data follow their respective retention rules and account controls. Support messages are kept while needed to resolve and follow up on your request, or where retention is legally required. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of support information we hold. We cannot directly delete data held by Apple or Google; use their linked privacy controls.

Children and changes

We do not request a child's name, age or contact details within the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address it. Advertising settings and store age ratings must be appropriate for the released app's audience.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
